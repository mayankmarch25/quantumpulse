{{ site.title }} is written by {{ site.author }}. It is not written by an expert. It is written by someone learning the subject from zero and refusing to move on from a concept until it can be drawn as something that moves.

Every edition takes one core concept and the single piece of maths it needs. The maths is introduced the week it is first used, never earlier, and never as a prerequisite list. The [maths track]({{ site.baseurl }}/maths/) and the [concepts track]({{ site.baseurl }}/concepts/) show what has been covered and what is next.

## How corrections work

Mistakes will happen. When one is found, the edition is fixed, a dated correction line is added at the bottom, and the person who caught it is credited if they want to be. Nothing is quietly patched.
