<h2>Why quantum, and why in the open</h2>
<p>My work is checking claims against data: what was announced versus what was delivered. Quantum computing has more big claims than any field I have seen, and I could not judge any of them. The only honest fix was to learn the maths properly, from the start. Writing it in the open keeps me honest: if I cannot draw an idea as a picture, I have not understood it yet.</p>
<p>The book is <em>Dancing with Qubits</em> by Robert S. Sutor, read in its own order. The <a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/#journey">plan</a> shows every edition.</p>
