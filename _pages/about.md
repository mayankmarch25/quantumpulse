<h2>Why quantum, and why in public</h2>
<p>I spend my working life testing claims against data: announced numbers versus delivered ones. Quantum computing is the most claim-heavy field I have met, and I could not evaluate any of it. The honest fix was to learn the maths properly, from the bottom. Writing it up in public is the discipline: a figure I cannot draw is an idea I do not yet understand.</p>
<p>The book is <em>Dancing with Qubits</em> by Robert S. Sutor, read in its own order. The <a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/#journey">journey</a> page shows every stop.</p>
