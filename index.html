03 / THE FORMAT

How every stop is built

Same shape each time, so you always know where you are inside an edition.

1

In one breath

Three lines at the top that say the whole idea. If you read nothing else, read those.

2

Pictures first

Four to six figures you can drag, flip or run. No symbol appears before the picture it describes.

3

Then the maths

The one or two rules the chapter needs, in a box at the bottom, each tied to a figure above.

4

Three things to remember

The edition ends with three sentences. Those are the test.
