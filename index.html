How this works

One book, read in public and in order: Dancing with Qubits. Its first half is the maths, its second half the quantum computing. The editions follow that order, one or two per chapter, and every maths edition ends by showing where that piece turns up in a qubit.

The rule: no symbol appears before the picture it describes, and no chapter is skipped.

What it is not

Not a course, not a news feed, and not on a schedule. I am a learner writing in public: editions land when a chapter is understood well enough to draw, roughly every two weeks. Some will be corrected later. When that happens the correction is printed, not quietly patched.

The risk: I will get things wrong. Tell me, and I will fix the edition and credit you.
